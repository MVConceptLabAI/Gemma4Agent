The problem

Precision and personality pull in opposite directions.

A useful caption must faithfully describe the same short video in four distinct voices. Rich language can invent details; overly cautious language can miss the scene. SceneGate separates seeing, verifying and writing so style never replaces evidence.

The objective is not merely fluent text. It is grounded text that remains recognizably factual in every required style.

Grounding policy

Agreement raises confidence. Conflict removes claims.

1
Cross-check: details reported by multiple observers are safe to retain.
2
Prune: isolated specific claims, uncertain text, colours and counts are dropped.
3
Write: Gemma 4 turns the supported record into clear style-specific captions.

This design targets the failure mode that matters most in captioning: a persuasive sentence that names something the video never showed.

Why Gemma 4

Gemma 4 gives the final writer a focused role: turn evidence into usable language.

Focused
One model is accountable for reconciling the scene record before writing.
Grounded
Prompts explicitly reject unseen intent, identity, text and background details.
Extensible
Gemma 4 image input can support a future visual-observer ablation without changing the contract.

The current release uses Gemma 4 31B as the final evidence-consolidation writer; future evaluation can measure its direct visual-observer role separately.
